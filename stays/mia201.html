A HOME, NOT JUST A ROOM

For all of us,and a little time out.

Gather around the generous dining table, then unwind in your own soaking tub. Warm wood, soft ivory and three bedrooms make Mia 201 a welcoming base for a shared Seoul escape.

This home is part of our Serenity Stay collection. We bring the practical comforts of home into your time in Korea: space to be together, a kitchen of your own, and an easy place to come back to.

Room for everyone.

Three bedrooms and six beds, including queen-size bunk beds. Two bathrooms make mornings easier for a larger group.

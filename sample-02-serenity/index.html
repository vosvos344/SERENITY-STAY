BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

A few things
worth knowing.

How do I make a reservation?+

Choose your stay and follow the link to Airbnb. You'll find live availability, the full price, and cancellation terms there. Your reservation and payment are completed on Airbnb.

When can I check in and out?+

Check-in is from 4 PM, and check-out is by 11 AM. Both Seoul stays offer self check-in. Your host will share arrival instructions through Airbnb.

Which stay is right for my group?+

Mia 201 has 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a private soaking tub, for up to 13 guests. Mia 202 has 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, for up to 7 guests. Confirm the guest count and applicable extra-person fees on Airbnb.

Is there anything else I should know?+

Our Seoul stays are smoke-free and do not allow pets or parties. Please keep noise down after 9 PM. You can find the complete house rules and contact the host on the Airbnb listing.

When does the Busan stay open?+

Our Busan chapter is still taking shape. An opening date and reservation link will be added here when they're ready.
